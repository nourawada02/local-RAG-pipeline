DMA reduces CPU involvement by handling raw data transfers directly via the controller without requiring constant polling or context switching [Source 1, Source 2]. While **programmed I/O** involves slow, continuous CPU polling and **interrupt-driven I/O** requires the CPU to stop execution for each transfer (context saving/restoring) [Source 3], DMA allows the data movement to occur at a much faster speed with minimal or no direct CPU intervention during the actual transfer process.
